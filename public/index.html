Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registers, waitlists, and badges.

When an event reaches full seating capacity, registering will place you in the queue. If a registered student cancels their registration, the first waitlisted student is automatically promoted, their QR ticket is generated, and a confirmation email is dispatched.

You earn 50 points by checking into an event, 50 points by passing quizzes, 100 points for a perfect quiz attempt, and 10 points per hour of volunteering. You can redeem these points in the Rewards Catalog for hoodies, water bottles, and accessories.
